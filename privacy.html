Data minimization

Privacy

This is a plain-language summary for a preview site. It is not a legal privacy policy for a formed nonprofit. TODO: have counsel review a real policy before launch. There is no staffed contact inbox yet.

This demo

The pages do not collect data. Forms do not submit. A parish or church card image does not upload. Nothing is written to storage in your browser. There is no account.

There are no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or other trackers. The site does not set a cookie for tracking. A content security policy blocks form submission and outside connections.

The rule we want later

Collect the smallest set of facts that lets the service run, say what that set is before anyone provides it, and delete the rest on a short clock.

Faith-community card

Consent comes before an upload. A live system would keep only a verified flag (yes or no), the parish or church name, and the time of the decision: { verified, parish_name, verified_at }.

The image would be stored encrypted, never displayed on a public page, and deleted within 24 hours after verification. If nobody verifies it, the plan is to delete it within 24 hours of upload anyway. The design notes are in docs/faith-card-verification.md. The demo keeps the image inside your browser and then forgets it when you leave.

Rides, gifts, and the waitlist

  • A live ride request would ask for what a ride credit needs (pickup, dropoff, riders). TODO: write the exact field list when a program exists. Phone numbers and payment data are not collected on this preview.
  • A donor’s name goes on the public leaderboard only if they opt in. The checkbox starts unchecked. Anonymous gifts would count toward a total without a name.
  • A Sober Driver Crew waitlist, when it exists, would store the name, email, and town someone chose to give. The demo form does not.

Selling data

The plan is to not sell personal information. This preview has none to sell.

Sample people

Rider, donor, and volunteer profiles marked SAMPLE are fictional. They are not records of a person.
