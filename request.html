How it will work

When the program is live, Safe Ride Home plans to cover the trip with a donor-funded ride credit or voucher. Free or discounted depends on the funds still available. This version has no drivers on staff, and a volunteer is not sent to pick you up.

Why you got this

Funds available covered 100% of this ride.

That sentence is a sample of the reason a future match would show. It does not describe your trip. This demo did not look at a fund balance, did not price a ride, and did not match you to a credit. The sentence stays the same no matter what you typed.

How that sentence gets chosen later

  1. Look at donor funds still available.
  2. Look at the price of a ride credit or voucher for the trip. TODO: that price has to come from the real program. It is not set on this site.
  3. If the funds cover the whole price, the ride is free and the reason reads: “Funds available covered 100% of this ride.”
  4. If the funds cover part of the price, the reason names the share covered and what is left to pay.
  5. If the funds cover none of it, the reason says the fund cannot cover a ride.
