Romantic Getaway

A date night, an anniversary, a weekend away. Round-trip safe rides for two, with the porch light on at both ends.

Who it’s for

The 40+ crowd. Couples heading out and heading home together.

What’s included

Ride credits for a round trip for two: one credit out, one credit back. How a live fund covers them — free or discounted — depends on the money on hand. The price of the package is not set.

Squad Ride

Friends get home together. A show, a game, a late dinner, a concert — then one larger vehicle, not five separate “I’ll be fine” drives.

Who it’s for

Friends 18 and up. This is a safe night out, not a drinking package. The legal drinking age is 21. Squad Ride does not include alcohol, and this page does not picture any.

What’s included

Ride credits for a larger vehicle so the group rides together. How many seats that vehicle has is not stated here. TODO: vehicle class comes from the ride-credit program. The price is not set.

New Driver Safe Pass

The license is the milestone. The pass is the backup: a covered ride home instead of driving impaired, or riding with someone who is.

Who it’s for

Someone getting a driver’s license, and the parent, family member, or sponsor who wants to preload the pass. Gift a pass from the sponsor demo.

What’s included

A bundle of ride credits the new driver can use to get home. The idea is that the ride home is covered. How many credits a pass holds, and what it costs to gift one, are not set.
