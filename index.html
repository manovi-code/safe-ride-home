Get home safe.

Safe Ride Home is a planned nonprofit so nobody drives drunk. You ask for a ride. Donors cover it with a ride credit or voucher — free or discounted, depending on the funds on hand. This first version has no drivers of our own.

How a ride gets covered

Three steps. No in-house drivers in this version.

1

You ask

Pickup, dropoff, and how many people are riding. The demo form stays on your device.

2

Funds decide the share

When this is live, you get a plain reason, such as “Funds available covered 100% of this ride,” a smaller share, or a clear no.

3

A credit covers the trip

A donor-funded ride credit or voucher pays for the ride. TODO: the program and its terms are not chosen yet.

Two more doors

Faith-community discount

Members of a parish, church, or other faith community will be able to use a card for a ride discount. The discount amount is not set. The demo never uploads the card.

See the faith-card demo

Sober Driver Crew

A later phase: volunteer sober drivers who help get someone’s own car home. It is not dispatching anyone. Insurance, background checks, and state rules come first.
