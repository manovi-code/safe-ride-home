Estimate method, in plain words

Estimated DUIs prevented is an estimate. It is rides home multiplied by a share of riders who would otherwise have driven impaired. Both inputs in this sample are placeholders.

estimated DUIs prevented (estimate)
  = sample rides home × placeholder share who would otherwise have driven impaired
  = 3 × 0.20
  = 0.6

sample miles covered
  = sample rides home × placeholder miles per ride
  = 3 × 8
  = 24

sample rides home
  = sample donation dollars ÷ placeholder dollars per ride credit
  = 75 ÷ 25
  = 3

Assumptions

  • Placeholder, not a sourced figure. One ride credit costs $25 in this sample. That is not a fare, not a quote, and not a company’s price. TODO: replace it with the real credit cost before any public stats.
  • Placeholder, not a sourced figure. Each sample ride is 8 miles. That is not a measured Rhode Island average. TODO: replace it with trip lengths from the actual program.
  • Placeholder, not a sourced figure. The share who would otherwise have driven impaired is 0.20 (1 in 5). It was picked so the formula is visible. It is not an official rate and not a Rhode Island rate. TODO: replace it with a sourced share, such as a survey of actual riders or a cited study.
