Faith-community discount

Devout members of a parish, church, or other faith community — Catholic and other faiths — will be able to show a card for a ride discount. The discount amount is not set.

What would be kept

When this is live, a completed check would store only:

  • a verified flag (yes or no)
  • the parish or church name
  • the time of that decision

The card image would be stored encrypted, never shown on a public page, and deleted within 24 hours after verification. The image would also be deleted within 24 hours of upload if nobody finishes the check.

In this demo, the image never leaves the browser. It is not uploaded and it is not stored. Any picture is enough for the preview — you do not need a real card.

The future design is written up in docs/faith-card-verification.md.
