Sober Driver Crew

This is the later idea: a volunteer sober driver helps get someone’s own car home, so the car and the people both get back without an impaired driver at the wheel.

It is not active for dispatch. It stays that way until insurance (including driving a third party’s vehicle), background checks, and Rhode Island rules are in place.

Dispatch is off

SOBER_CREW_DISPATCH_ENABLED is false. Volunteer drivers are not dispatched. The flag may only be turned on after Mann confirms in writing that insurance (including driving a third party’s vehicle), background checks, and Rhode Island rules are in place.

The switch lives in js/flags.js as SOBER_CREW_DISPATCH_ENABLED = false.
